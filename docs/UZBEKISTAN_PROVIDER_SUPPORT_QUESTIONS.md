## Do not send yet / security checklist

Before opening an account:
- Read the current provider agreement and privacy policy on the official domain.
- Verify the domain and support email/phone independently.
- Do not send identity documents through Telegram, WhatsApp or ordinary email unless the provider's official verified process explicitly requires it.
- Never disclose a password, SMS/OTP code, SSH private key, WireGuard private key or a client `.conf` file to support.
- Do not add a payment card or wallet balance while our €0 constraint remains.
- Keep the provider's written answer about WireGuard, UDP, IPv4 egress and post-trial billing.

Ask **UNICON first** because the public promotion currently advertises 30 free days without obligations. Ask **ServerPlus second** because its 100,000 UZS promo credit and WireGuard FAQ are more directly documented, but identity verification and credit expiry make it a more sensitive route.
